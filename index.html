During this time, I conducted research under the supervision of Prof. Guangtao Zhai and Dr. Jianbo Zhang, with close mentorship from Chunyi Li. I am now a junior at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, majoring in Industrial and Operations Engineering as part of the UM–SJTU dual-degree program, working with Prof. Liyue Shen and Lixuan Chen. My research interests include computer vision, multimodal large models, and AI for Science.
